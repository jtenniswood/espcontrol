# Appearance

Open **Screen > Appearance** on your [device setup page](/features/setup). Choose a setting below.

## Active Colour

Choose the default highlight for active cards or customise individual card colours. [Set active colours](/settings/active-colour).

## Card Style

Choose Small, Medium, or Large card curves. [Change card style](/settings/card-style).

## Theme

Choose Dark, Light, or an automatic day/night theme. [Set the theme](/settings/theme).

## Idle

Return from a popup or subpage to home after inactivity. [Set the idle timeout](/features/idle).

## Clock Bar

Show or hide the top bar and its night-mode indicator. [Configure the clock bar](/features/clock-bar).

For one card's label or icon, use [Layout settings](/settings/layout). For brightness or orientation, open [Display](/settings/display).
