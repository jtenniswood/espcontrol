# 10.1-inch JC8012P4A1

The **Guition JC8012P4A1** is a 10.1-inch touchscreen powered by an **ESP32-P4** processor and ESP32-C6 WiFi co-processor. EspControl uses it in landscape orientation, with a 1280x800 layout and room for **20 cards** on the home screen.

Choose the page for your panel version before installing. The rear-case revisions look very similar and the newer one may not say `V2` on the label.

::: warning Confirm production silicon before using case dates
- If ESPHome logs or `esptool` chip information reports **ESP32-P4 v3.x production silicon**, use the **V3 firmware** regardless of the rear-case date or label.
- Rear case marking `2627` or lower: use **V1 / original panel** firmware.
- Rear case marking `2628` or higher: use **V2 / new panel** firmware.

Case dates are only a fallback for panels whose chip information does not identify V3 silicon.
:::
